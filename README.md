# PakHelp AI – Pakistani Citizen Assistant

## Overview

PakHelp AI is an AI-powered citizen assistance system designed specifically for Pakistan. The platform aims to simplify access to government services, document understanding, utility bill analysis, career guidance, emergency assistance, and language translation for citizens.

The primary goal of PakHelp AI is to bridge the information gap between Pakistani citizens and essential public services by providing simple, accurate, and easy-to-understand guidance in both Urdu and English.

Whether a user wants to apply for a CNIC, understand an electricity bill, find scholarship opportunities, translate documents, or seek emergency guidance, PakHelp AI provides step-by-step assistance through an intelligent and user-friendly interface.

---

## Problem Statement

Many Pakistani citizens face difficulties while:

* Understanding government procedures.
* Filling official forms and applications.
* Reading utility bills and official notices.
* Finding scholarships and career opportunities.
* Accessing emergency information.
* Understanding technical or legal terminology.
* Translating Urdu and English documents accurately.

PakHelp AI solves these problems by providing centralized AI-powered assistance in simple language.
